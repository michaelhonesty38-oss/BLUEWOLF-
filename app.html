Test this with two phones
  1. Open the app on both phones. Sign up as a customer on one and as a driver on the other, or use Switch at the top to change sides.
  2. On the customer phone, order a ride or delivery.
  3. On the driver phone, tap Go online, then Accept.
  4. Allow location when the phone asks. The customer and driver then see each other on the map during the trip.
  5. Both people must have Contributor access to the page, or orders will not save. Without it, the app runs on one phone only, and you can play both sides yourself.

Fares are examples. Real prices will be set after the pilot. Maps by OpenStreetMap contributors.
