The pilot

Start at Issele-Azagba Junction, then expand across Asaba

Proposed corridor · demand and fares need field validation

Proposed pilot area

Issele-Azagba Junction on the Asaba–Benin Expressway

Blue Wolf concentrates its first drivers and daily commuters around this corridor instead of launching across the whole city.

Junction→Adjoining Asaba routes→Wider city coverage→More cities and delivery later

Launch sequence

  1. Map the main commuter destinations from the junction.
  2. Measure passenger volumes, current fares and peak waiting times.
  3. Recruit and position drivers around the operating area.
  4. Run a controlled pilot in peak commuting periods.
  5. Measure pickup time, completion and repeat use.
  6. Expand to adjoining routes once pilot targets are met.

Supply track

  • Recruit local drivers
  • Verify driver and vehicle
  • Explain commission and protection
  • Position supply around peak periods
  • Run 2–3 company vehicles to cover peak-time gaps

Demand track

  • Target one commuter corridor
  • Reach workplaces and residential clusters
  • Run local referral activity
  • Convert first riders into repeat users
